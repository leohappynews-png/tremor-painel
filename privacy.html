Privacy Policy

AURA (BR Academy Ltd, trading as AURA) · Dublin, Ireland · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy covers the AURA internal publishing tool (the "AURA Postiz" app), which we use to schedule and publish content on social media accounts that belong to AURA and its brands (for example @spotnews.ie, @bracademy.ie and @weareaura.ie). It is not offered to the public.

What data we access

When an account owner connects a Facebook Page or Instagram professional account, Meta gives us an access token and basic account information (account ID, name, profile picture). With the owner's permission we can publish posts, read the comments and the insights (reach, engagement) of our own accounts.

How we use it

Only to publish and manage content on our own accounts and to measure how that content performs. We do not use the data for advertising profiles, we do not sell it and we do not share it with third parties, except the hosting provider that runs the tool on our behalf (servers in the European Union).

Comments and messages from other people

Comments on our posts may be read and answered by our team. We do not store them outside Meta's platforms for any other purpose.

How long we keep it

Access tokens are kept while the account stays connected. Disconnecting the account in the tool, or removing the app in Facebook settings, revokes access and deletes the token from our systems.

Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete any data we hold about you, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). To request deletion of data related to this app, email helloauraie@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and we will confirm within 30 days. You can also remove the app at any time in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.
